Halo in the browser

Halo: Combat Evolved, running in WebAssembly from the open-source Xbox decompilation. Free to play, no disc needed, with split-screen and online multiplayer.

Step 1 · Controller

Connect your Switch Pro Controller

Pair it, then press any button. It should light up below. Mouse and keyboard also work (WASD, mouse, click to fire).

How to connect (USB-C or Bluetooth)
  • Easiest, for Switch 1 and Switch 2 Pro Controllers: plug it into the Mac with a USB-C cable and press a button. No pairing needed.
  • Bluetooth, Switch 1 Pro Controller: hold the small round sync button on top (next to the USB-C port) until the four lights run back and forth. On the Mac: System Settings → Bluetooth → Pro Controller → Connect.
  • Bluetooth, Switch 2 Pro Controller: hold the sync button until the lights pulse, then connect from System Settings → Bluetooth. If it pairs but doesn't show up above, use the USB-C cable.
  • Browsers hide controllers until the first button press, so press one, then reload this page if nothing appears.
  • Still nothing? Remove the controller in Bluetooth settings and pair it again. Keep it connected to one device at a time (turn your Switch off).
  • Chrome and Safari both read the controller through the standard Gamepad API; no drivers or apps needed.

Step 2 · Play

Launch Halo

The game is hosted by its developer, Mitchell Hynes. Use a current desktop Chrome (recommended); Safari may work but isn't officially supported.

Playing with a friend
  • In the game choose Play online, pick a map and mode, and create a private lobby.
  • Copy the invite link and send it to your friend. They open it and join automatically; you start when everyone is ready.
  • For couch co-op on one Mac, plug in a second controller for split-screen.
  • Audio starts muted. Use the sound toggle in the game page.
