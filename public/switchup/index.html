switchup

this app lives on your menu bar and lets you control your computer with a nintendo switch controller. setup time is less than 2 minutes. i spent a lot of time dialing in the optimal controls. dictation, backspace, and switching desktops are all supported. with practice, it's possible to fully replace mouse and keyboard. check out the default controls:

left stickmove cursor
right stickscroll (two-finger, swipe notifications away)
a / bleft click / right click
xclose tab
yspotlight
l / rprevious / next tab
zl / zrprevious / next desktop
+dictation (hold to talk)
−backspace a word
homereturn
captureesc
d-pad ← / →switch apps (⌘⇥)
d-pad ↑ / ↓mission control / show desktop
l3 / r3precision mode / address bar
