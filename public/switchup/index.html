switchup

download github

this app lives on your menu bar and lets you control your computer with a nintendo switch controller. setup time is less than 2 minutes. i spent around 4 hours dialing in the optimal controls. dictation, backspace, and switching desktops are all supported. with practice, it's possible to fully replace mouse and keyboard.

it's super lightweight, just ~1,400 lines of code. notarized by apple via my developer id, so it's been scanned for malware.

click into the left joystick to toggle precision mode (very useful). the d-pad is also very useful. honestly, all of these controls are pretty dialed in. however, you may remap as you please!
